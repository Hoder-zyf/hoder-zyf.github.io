Hi, I am Yifei Zhang (张逸飞), a master's student in Financial Engineering and Fintech at Nanjing University's School of Management & Engineering, advised by Prof. Honghai Yu. I hold a bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from the Department of Computer Science and Technology at Nanjing University, where I specialized in Computer & Financial Engineering Experimental Class (a Top Talent Program).

I am currently a research intern at Century Frontier, working on AutoResearch in the quantitative area. Before that, I was a research intern at XYZ AI LAB from April 2026 to July 2026, advised by Xiao Yang and Jiang Bian, focusing on AI4AI research on LLM Post-training. Before that, I was a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia (MSRA) from May 2025 to January 2026, advised by Xiao Yang, focusing on Autonomous Agent research. Previously, I had the privilege of collaborating with Prof. Benyou Wang and Prof. Qianqian Xie on exploring Large Language Models in Finance.

I have published at top-tier conferences including NeurIPS, ICML, and ACL. Notably, one of my papers received the Best Paper Award at the ICLR 2025 Workshop on Advances in Financial AI, where I presented it as an oral presentation in Singapore. My research interests are AI4AI and Autonomous Agents.

🎯 Open to opportunities: I am currently on the job market for full-time positions. If you think I would be a good fit, feel free to reach out!

Reasoning as Gradient: Scaling MLE Agents Beyond Tree Search (MLE Setting of RD-Agent)
Yifei Zhang*, Xu Yang*, Xiao Yang*, Bowen Xian, Qizheng Li, et al.
ACL Findings 2026
The MLE setting of RD-Agent, where Gome sets the strongest open-source MLE result on MLE-Bench under a closed-world protocol. Its main finding is that as model reasoning capabilities improve, gradient-based optimization increasingly outperforms gradient-free tree search.
