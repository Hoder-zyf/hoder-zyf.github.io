I'm a master's student in Financial Engineering and Fintech at Nanjing University, advised by Prof. Honghai Yu. I also earned my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Nanjing University.

At Century Frontier, I'm an Agentic Quant Research Intern working on AutoResearch for quantitative finance. I'm interested in how autonomous agents can carry out research, a question that connects my work in AI4AI and FinTech.

As a research assistant at CUHK-Shenzhen, I studied large language models for finance with Prof. Benyou Wang. I then studied autonomous agents with Xiao Yang during an internship at Microsoft Research Asia. At XYZ AI LAB, Xiao Yang and Jiang Bian advised my research on LLM post-training.

I've published at NeurIPS, ICML, and ACL. One of my papers won the Best Paper Award at the ICLR 2025 Workshop on Advances in Financial AI, where I gave an oral presentation in Singapore.

AI4AI at Scale: A Full-Pipeline System for Enhancing LLM Agentic Capabilities

XYZ Agentic Team
XYZ AI LAB Technical Report, 2026
TL;DR A bounded-exploration, verification-gated framework for improving complete agentic systems, instantiated in XYZ-Aquila, a family of open-weight Deep Search agents. XYZ-Aquila-pro records the highest reported score among all listed systems on BrowseComp-ZH, LiveBrowseComp, and WideSearch.
PDF 🤗 Model Harness GitHub stars

RD-Agent Project

A unified research line on autonomous ML engineering agents. The technical report defines the RD-Agent framework, while the two follow-up papers study how the same project scales to Machine Learning Engineering (MLE) optimization and autonomous LLM fine-tuning.
RD-Agent Project GitHub stars

R&D-Agent: An LLM-Agent Framework Towards Autonomous Data Science (Technical Report)

Xu Yang, Xiao Yang, Shikai Fang, Yifei Zhang, Jian Wang, et al.
arXiv preprint 2025
A top-performing open-source framework on MLE-Bench that structures autonomous data science into two parts: Research for proposing ideas and Development for turning them into executable solutions.
arXiv

Reasoning as Gradient: Scaling MLE Agents Beyond Tree Search (MLE Setting of RD-Agent)

Yifei Zhang*, Xu Yang*, Xiao Yang*, Bowen Xian, Qizheng Li, et al.
ACL Findings 2026
The MLE setting of RD-Agent, where Gome sets the strongest open-source MLE result on MLE-Bench under a closed-world protocol. Its main finding is that as model reasoning capabilities improve, gradient-based optimization increasingly outperforms gradient-free tree search.
arXiv 🤗 Dataset

FT-Dojo: Towards Autonomous LLM Fine-Tuning with Language Agents (Fine-Tuning Setting of RD-Agent)

Qizheng Li*, Yifei Zhang*, Xiao Yang*, Xu Yang*, Zhuo Wang, et al.
ICML 2026
The fine-tuning setting of RD-Agent, introducing an interactive benchmark with 13 tasks across 5 domains and FT-Agent, which achieves the best results on 10 of 13 tasks.
arXiv

TwinMarket: A Scalable Behavioral and Social Simulation for Financial Markets

Yuzhe Yang*, Yifei Zhang*, Minghao Wu*, Kaidi Zhang, Yunmiao Zhang, et al.
NeurIPS 2025 🏆 Best Paper Award @ ICLR 2025 Workshop on Advances in Financial AI (1/53)
TL;DR A multi-agent simulation platform for financial markets that integrates behavioral economics and social network dynamics, enabling large-scale market experiments with diverse agent behaviors.
